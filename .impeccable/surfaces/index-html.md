# Surface: KNC Studio home (index.html)

Mode: Persuade, with an Experience segment (the project ring).
Audience: decision-makers at companies commissioning a website; Turkish; phone-first.
Job: believe KNC Studio builds working products, then scope a project and send it.
Proof: delivered tour-booking platform (client named, name pending); screenshots and screen recording pending from the user.
Constraints: no prices anywhere; pricing "after a meeting to understand all your needs". Hero wireframe scene and curved project ring stay as built.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The plan is the schedule. After the ring, services are not a list of offers but rows on a week ruler; ticking one draws its bar and moves the finish line. Refuses the agency default of service cards, an icon row and a generic contact form.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black #0f0f0d ground, cream #efe9dc text, one muted gold #c9ab70 for selected state, bars and the finish line, warm #1a1713 for filled surfaces. Cormorant Garamond for every name and number that matters; Inter 300 for body; small tracked uppercase for ruler ticks and meta. Hairline rules, dashed = not yet, solid gold = chosen (the hero's wireframe grammar).

STORY: Visitor sees the tour platform proof (what was built, grouped Ödeme / Operasyon / Otomasyon), reads the process, then builds their own plan on the schedule, adds their own ideas, describes their business, and emails the plan. Price is a meeting, never a number.

FIRST VIEWPORT: (of the planner) Serif title "Ne kadar sürer?" top-left with an italic one-line instruction; a full-width week ruler; one row per service, checkbox and serif name on the left third, gold bar on the ruler; a vertical gold finish line labelled "~ X–Y hafta" in italic serif; footer strip with the no-price sentence left and the outlined "Planı e-postayla gönder" action right.

FORM: The schedule, position 2 of 3 on the dealt hand (chosen after one re-roll). seed:6b44b18599a8dc38
Signature interaction: ticking a row draws its bar (scaleX, heavy ease) and slides the finish line; unticked rows preview a dashed ghost bar at the current finish.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
